FIELD GUIDE · enemies, bosses and mode rules

DELIVERY CONTRACT

Find the gold CARGO crate, carry it to the flag (or claim the boss reward). Thieves can knock it loose: recover the dropped crate. Cargo never reduces movement or weapon strength.

Three separate medals per stage: CLEAR, SPEED, CARGO. Earn them across different runs. First cargo delivery pays 25 base coins (Double Coins applies); delivery grades are GOLD (speed target) or SILVER. Speed uses active play time, not bought timer refills.

Enemy tactics: blue shields block one frontal hit, then stagger briefly; flank or follow up. Rooftop throwers lob projectiles. Gold-marked thieves steal cargo on contact. Regional bosses warn for one second before their signature attacks and intensify below 40% health.
